# Authoritative Source Material Ingestion Policy

## Chapter 1: Ingestion Criteria & Provenance Standards

### Section 1.1: Criteria for Authoritative Sources
This directory is strictly reserved for genuine, verified external source material obtained under valid licenses or public regulatory frameworks. To maintain the scientific and operational integrity of WindGuard AI, zero unverified or speculative documents may be placed in this repository.

### Section 1.2: Strict Prohibition of Unverified Materials
Material whose authorship, provenance, version, or publication date cannot be independently validated is classified as UNVERIFIED and is strictly prohibited from entering the authoritative corpus. All project-authored summaries must reside in the derived/ directory, and all test scenarios must reside in synthetic/.
